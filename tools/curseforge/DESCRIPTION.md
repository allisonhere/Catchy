## Description

# Reel Catchy

Reel Catchy (addon folder `Catchy`, commands `/ca` and `/catchy`) keeps track of the fish you catch and handles the fiddly parts of fishing: gear, lures and double-click casting. It also shows you how well you're actually doing.

> Reel Catchy is an unofficial fork of [Fishing Buddy](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/fishing-buddy), by Sutorix and the many people who built it over the years.

## Fishing made easy
- Double-click to cast, including the "fish without a pole" feature
- Pick a fishing outfit and swap to it, and back, with one command
- Lures are applied to your pole automatically
- Fishing pets and drinks, for the full experience

## Know your catch
- **Fish Watcher:** the fish you've caught this session and at this spot before
- **Session dashboard:** catch rate, misses, streaks, catches per hour and gold earned
- **Fishing Journal:** a 14-day catch chart, top catches, favorite waters and performance stats
- **Rhythm analytics:** rolling catch rate, average bite time, cadence, interrupted and unlooted casts
- **Cycle fish tracking**, such as Nightfin Snapper

## Find the pools
- **World map overlay:** known fishing pools, colored by how recently you saw them
- **Minimap pool radar:** live nearby pools with freshness colors, and it works with a rotating minimap

## Works with Tempus
Reel Catchy has optional [Tempus](TEMPUS_LINK) integration. If you have Tempus installed, all of Catchy's settings pages, including the Journal, Locations and Outfits, appear in Tempus's options window under their own Catchy section, using your Tempus accent color. Without Tempus, Catchy uses its own settings window. Nothing else changes.

## Commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/ca` or `/catchy` | Open Catchy settings |
| `/ca switch` | Wear your fishing outfit, or switch back |
| `/ca journal` | Open the Journal |
| `/ca stats` | Toggle the session dashboard |
| `/ca radar` | Toggle the minimap pool radar |
| `/ca help` | List all commands |

## Known issues
"Fast Loot" addons can interfere with fish counting, because the loot disappears before Catchy can record it.
